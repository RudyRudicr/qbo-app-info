Privacy Policy

QuickBooks Expense Automation

Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Overview

QuickBooks Expense Automation is a private, internal-use application for business expense processing and accounting integration. It is associated with Ocala Handyman Near Me LLC and is not available for public use.

2. Information Accessed

When authorized, the application may access QuickBooks Online information including:

  • Company identification information
  • Chart of accounts and account identifiers
  • Purchase, expense, and refund transactions
  • Transaction dates, amounts, and descriptions
  • Other accounting information necessary for authorized expense processing

The application also processes transaction records provided directly by authorized users, including purchase and refund exports from retailers.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used exclusively for internal business accounting purposes, including:

  • Categorizing business expenses
  • Matching purchases and refunds
  • Identifying duplicate transactions
  • Preparing accounting reports and proposals
  • Reviewing existing QuickBooks transactions
  • Supporting authorized accounting operations

4. Storage and Security

The application is designed to operate locally on authorized computers rather than through a publicly accessible application server.

Local files may include retailer transaction data, accounting reports, configuration information, and processing history.

QuickBooks authentication credentials and tokens are maintained separately from publicly accessible website content and source-code repositories. Access to local information is restricted to authorized users.

5. Sharing of Information

QuickBooks information is processed for authorized internal business purposes. The application is not intended to sell accounting data or share it for advertising or marketing purposes.

Information is exchanged with Intuit QuickBooks Online as necessary to provide the authorized integration.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored transaction records, reports, and processing history may be retained as necessary for accounting, auditing, troubleshooting, and applicable recordkeeping obligations.

Authorized users may request deletion of locally stored application data by contacting the application administrator. Deletion requests will be handled subject to applicable legal and accounting recordkeeping requirements.

7. QuickBooks Authorization

QuickBooks access is granted through Intuit's OAuth authorization process. Authorized QuickBooks administrators may revoke the application's access through QuickBooks.

Once authorization is revoked, the application must discontinue access to the disconnected QuickBooks company.

8. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application functionality or data-handling practices change.
